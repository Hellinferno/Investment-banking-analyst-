## Entry requirements

The event closes **10 October 2026 at 23:59 IST**. Participants must meet the rules' India residency, age 18+, team-size (1–5), and exclusions requirements; this plan does not establish your eligibility.

Existing projects are permitted with disclosure of prior work and identifiable new contributions. SerpApi must materially support the core function. AI-assisted work is permitted with tool/contribution disclosure. Respect third-party rights; an open-source license is encouraged, not mandatory.

Provide a public GitHub repository with runnable setup and an explanation of SerpApi usage. Supply a screen recording shorter than three minutes through an accessible public or unlisted link. Complete the authenticated dashboard entry, required participant/project information, disclosures, and terms acceptance, then actually submit rather than leave a draft.

## Track decision

Choose **Commerce & Market Intelligence**: the central use case is company and transaction research for analysts. This is a recommendation based on product purpose, not an organizer endorsement. Keep the product story consistent with that track.

## Turn the five judging criteria into evidence

The organizer does not publish percentage weights in the checked rules. The proof choices below are our strategy.

| Criterion | What this project should prove | Concrete proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea strength | A clear problem: fragmented early company research | One analyst task explained in 15 seconds |
| Originality | Evidence becomes a reviewable next action | Persistent review board and exported handoff, if completed |
| Technical complexity | Search orchestration works under real constraints | Query plan, provenance, partial failure, auth and saved-run behavior |
| Usefulness | Another person can review and reuse the research | Source links, analyst notes, approval and PDF/XLSX |
| Meaningful SerpApi usage | Removing search removes the core evidence | A real run without an LLM, followed by cited artifacts |

A technically ambitious feature that fails during the demo weakens this evidence. A polished, truthful workflow is the preferred final-sprint investment.

## Submission decisions

Human decisions: personal eligibility, participant information, terms acceptance, final merge, and submission. Codex should prepare the materials and identify gaps. Do not change the repository license without the owner's decision; inspect existing licenses and acknowledge dependencies.
